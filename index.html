A Letter From Me

My Baby,

I don't say this enough, so I want to say it slowly, in writing, where it can't get lost in a busy day: thank you. Thank you for the small things you probably don't even notice,the way you motivate me, the way you laugh at my worst jokes like they're my best ones, the way you make ordinary days feel like they matter.

Loving you has taught me that the best parts of my life aren't the big, loud moments. They're the quiet ones, falling asleep on a phone call, the playlists/ songs we share, the letter you made about why you love me. You've made a home out of the everyday, and I never want to take that for granted.

I'm so grateful for who you are: your patience, your honesty, the way you care for the people you love without ever asking for anything back. You make me want to be better, kinder, steadier, more present, simply by being yourself around me.

I can't wait for everything still ahead of us. New places, inside jokes we haven't made yet, ordinary Tuesdays and the kind of history that only comes from choosing each other, again and again. Whatever this year brings, I hope it brings more of us.

Happy Girlfriend's Day, my love.
Forever yours
